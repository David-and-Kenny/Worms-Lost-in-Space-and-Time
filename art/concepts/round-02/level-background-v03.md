## Changes

Restored V01-like framing with large cropped corner cogs, pipe elbows, collars,
and mechanical panels. Background gears use dull brown/rust tones rather than
the gameplay concept's bright gold. Pipework uses muted weathered red-brown,
with simple patina patches and subdued metal highlights. The smaller window
shows only space, sparse stars, and a small planet. Broad blue-gray wall panels
keep the middle calmer.

The round-01 playable cog was supplied as a color-separation reference, not an
element to insert into the background.

## Validation and limits

Visually checked the restored machinery, dull rustic palette, smaller window,
and absence of cogs in its view. This is a flattened background concept, not
separate layers. Separation from actual gameplay, portrait cropping, and
readability at device size still require in-level testing. Production artwork,
the character, scenes, physics, GUIDs, and bindings are unchanged.
