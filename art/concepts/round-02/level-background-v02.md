## Owner feedback

The owner likes the background and the window into space, but says the
background machinery will conflict with the actual cog assets on screen.
Requested a smaller window and no random cogs visible through it.

## Changes

Reduced the space window to a smaller inset porthole. Removed decorative gears
both inside and outside the window. Replaced the busy machine frame with muted
blue-gray hull panels and narrow copper perimeter pipework. Space now contains
only a small planet and sparse stars. The existing cartoon style is retained.

The broader removal of decorative gears addresses the stated competition with
playable cogs. The owner has not yet reviewed these exact changes.

## Validation and limits

Visually checked for the smaller window, absence of gears, simpler composition,
and retained cartoon style. The revision was not rendered behind actual gameplay.
Phone cropping, window placement against level routes, and player/asset contrast
remain to be checked before production integration. Original game artwork and
character assets are unchanged.
