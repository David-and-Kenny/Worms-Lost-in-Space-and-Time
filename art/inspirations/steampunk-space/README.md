# Steampunk and space references

## Direction from the project owner

"I like the steampunk and space aspect combination. The game is heavily focused
around gear cogs and space pipey steampunk aesthetic."

This statement establishes the thematic direction. These eight images provide
references for cogs, clockwork mechanisms, pipework, spacecraft, and industrial
interiors. It does not approve every detail, a photorealistic rendering style, or
an exact color palette.

## Combining the references

- Start with cogs as prominent, readable forms: teeth, spokes, circular cutouts,
  layered hubs, and varied diameters.
- Explore pipes as a recurring construction language: bends, flanges, collars,
  valves, and pressure vessels.
- Use the spacecraft images to connect that machinery to outer space through
  portholes, hull sections, stars, and planets.
- Explore brass, copper, worn iron, warm work lights, and cool glass as a candidate
  palette drawn from these references. Final colors remain undecided.
- Adapt the detail to the game's mobile view. Keep the character, playable
  surfaces, and hazards easy to distinguish from background machinery.

These are art suggestions. The references do not introduce new gameplay,
physics, hazards, or a requirement that illustrated gears physically mesh.
